# DoseBell privacy policy

Effective 26 September 2026

## In short

DoseBell keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you leaves the phone unless you set up caregiver alerts, share a report, or your phone makes an encrypted backup.

## What DoseBell keeps on your phone

The names you enter for the people taking medicines; each medicine's name, amount, times, days, start date and length, food rules and written instructions; the photos and voice instructions you add; when each dose was taken, skipped, snoozed or missed; the change log of every change to a prescription; and your caregiver settings.

All of it is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Uninstalling DoseBell deletes it.

## What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

Caregiver alerts (optional). If you set up a caregiver, DoseBell sends short messages to that person's phone: the patient's name, the medicine's name, the dose time, and whether a dose was missed or skipped or a medicine was added, changed, stopped or removed. They are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to ntfy.sh, a free public notification service run by a third party, which delivers them to the caregiver's ntfy app and keeps them for up to about 12 hours. Anyone who has the invite link can read these messages, so share it only with your caregiver. "Stop alerts" ends them at once; setting up again creates a new private link.

Reports you share. The medication report is made on your phone as a PDF and goes only where you send it.

Backups. If your phone backs up apps, DoseBell's medicines, history, photos and voice instructions are included only when the backup is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock, so they can be restored on a new phone. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

## Permissions and why they are needed

Notifications, alarms & reminders and full-screen alerts: to ring at each dose time, over the lock screen.

Camera: only when you choose to take a photo of a medicine. Photos picked from your gallery come through Android's photo picker, which gives DoseBell only the photo you choose.

Microphone: only while you record a voice instruction.

Internet: only to send caregiver alerts.

Running at start-up and in the background: to set the alarms again after a restart.

## Children

DoseBell is meant to be set up by adults. It is not directed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone.

## Deleting your data

A medicine never taken can be deleted with its photo and voice instruction. One that was taken is removed from the list but kept in History, so the record of what was taken cannot be quietly erased. Uninstalling DoseBell deletes everything on the phone. Caregiver messages expire from ntfy.sh by themselves.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on its Play Store page, with a new date. Questions can be sent to the developer's email address shown on DoseBell's Play Store page.
