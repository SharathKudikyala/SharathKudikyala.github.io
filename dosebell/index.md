# DoseBell

Medicine reminders for elderly people and the families who look after them.

- A full-screen alarm at each dose time, with the medicine's photo and a family member's recorded instructions
- Doses marked taken, skipped or missed, and a one-page report to show the doctor
- Optional alerts to a caregiver's phone when a dose is missed or medicines change
- English, Telugu, Hindi, Marathi, Malayalam, Kannada and Tamil

Everything stays on the phone: no account, no advertising, no tracking.
