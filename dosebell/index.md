# DoseBell

Medicine reminders, health readings and records, for you and the ones you care for, on iPhone and Android.

- An alarm at each dose time, with the medicine's photo and a family member's recorded instructions; it starts with a short vibration and grows from soft to full
- Doses marked taken, skipped or missed, and a one-page report to show the doctor
- Optional alerts to a caregiver's phone when a dose is missed or medicines change
- Health readings such as blood pressure and sugar, plus water, walks and sleep
- Doctors, appointments with reminders, and lab reports and prescriptions kept as photos or PDFs, ready to share
- An Emergency card to show or share: blood group, allergies and people to call
- Scan a prescription to add its medicines (beta)
- 17 languages, including English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Spanish, French, Arabic and Chinese

Everything stays on the phone: no account, no advertising, no tracking.

DoseBell is a reminder, not medical advice: always follow your doctor or pharmacist.
