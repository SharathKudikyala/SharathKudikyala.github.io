Reminders

An alarm you cannot miss, with each medicine's photo

At each dose time DoseBell rings with a photo of the real medicine, how much to take, and whether it goes before or after food.

  • Starts with a short vibration, then the sound grows from soft to full
  • Reads the instruction aloud, or plays a family member's voice note
  • Taken, Snooze or Skip; rings again if it is ignored
  • Tablets, halves, syrups, drops, inhalers, injections and more
Today

The whole day's medicines at a glance

See what has been taken, what is next and when, in the order it is due. Every medicine shows its time, food rule and how many tablets are left.

Family alerts · optional

A son or daughter is told if a dose is missed

Alerts go to a family member's phone through the free ntfy app. No account and no phone number are needed.

  • Missed doses, after 30 or 60 minutes
  • Skipped doses, as soon as they are skipped
  • Medicines added, changed, stopped or removed

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no account and no login. Everything you add stays on the phone.

Does the family member need DoseBell too?

No. They install the free ntfy app and open the invite you send. They are told on their phone when a dose is missed or skipped, or when medicines change.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. On Android the alarm opens full screen, even over the lock screen. On iPhone it rings as a time-sensitive notification with DoseBell's chime; tapping it opens the alarm screen.

Can one phone look after more than one person?

Yes. Each person has their own profile and medicines, and the alarm always shows whose dose it is.

The alarm does not ring on my Android phone. Why?

Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. In DoseBell, open the battery tip and allow it to run in the background; the app shows the steps for your phone.

Is DoseBell medical advice?

No. DoseBell is a reminder and record keeper, not a medical device. It does not recommend any medicine, dose or timing. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
